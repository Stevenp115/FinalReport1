The idea of running the KF in reduced-dimension PCA space is a proven method, as described in a recent review by \textcite{Li_Li_Gao_Gao_2026}. Indeed, a single prediction-update step for the standard KF has cubic time complexity in the state dimension, $p$ \cite{schmidt2024rankreducedkalmanfilterapproximate}. As such, if $p$ is large, this motivates the idea of perfoming our filtering in a $q$-dimensional space, where $q\ll p$, and then try to recover our state estimates in the original $p$-dimensional space.
